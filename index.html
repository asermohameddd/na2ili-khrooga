HOW IT WORKS

Budget in.
Khrooga out.

No booking database. No giant form. Just a smart front-end planner that helps you decide what to do with the money you already have.

01

Set your group

Tell us how many people are going, your average amount per person, and the exact group total.

02

Get budget-close results

Results are ranked by distance from your target, so a 500 EGP budget starts with roughly 450–550 EGP ideas instead of random cheap options.

03

Build the day

The package section uses the same budget target to put the closest full-day plans first.

₤

Price accuracy comes first

Ranges are planning estimates for Cairo and Greater Cairo. Public prices can change by branch, ticket type, promotion, season and date, so the site uses narrow planning ranges and avoids pretending to be a live checkout.
